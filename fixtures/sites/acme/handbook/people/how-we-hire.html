How we hire

Our interview process is designed to mirror the job. After an initial recruiter screen, candidates complete a take-home exercise (about four hours) focused on a realistic frontend problem. The onsite then includes a system-design round for a high-traffic product surface, plus behavioural conversations about mentorship and collaboration.

  1. Recruiter screen
  2. Take-home exercise
  3. System-design interview
  4. Behavioural / values interview
